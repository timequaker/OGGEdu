Ongoing Education

OGG Edu Journal Clubs

Throughout the year, OGG Edu hosts a series of journal clubs — closed-door, discussion-based reviews of the current GI literature. Each journal club brings prescribers together to examine the evidence and talk through how it applies to everyday patient care.

Per Year
8 Journal Clubs
Attendance
20–30 Prescribers
Format
Closed-Door Literature Review
What to Expect

A focused, collegial discussion

LIT

Review of the Literature

Each journal club is built around a close reading of recent peer-reviewed literature, giving attendees a clear view of where the evidence stands.

MD

Local & Regional Experts

We often welcome local and regional experts in gastroenterology, surgery, and gynecology to join the discussion and add their perspectives.

20+

Small, Closed-Door Setting

With typically 20 to 30 prescribers in the room, the setting stays intimate and interactive, with plenty of room for questions and open dialogue.

Event Formats

Two ways to take part

Single-Sponsor Journal Clubs

Held five to six times a year, these events are supported by a single sponsor.

Multi-Sponsor Journal Clubs

Held two to three times a year, these events are supported by multiple sponsors.

For Sponsors

Sponsor Meet & Greet

Sponsors are invited to a 30-minute meet and greet with attendees immediately before each journal club begins, an opportunity to connect with prescribers in a relaxed setting before the discussion starts.
