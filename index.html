What We Do

Educational programs for GI providers

GI

Symposia

Held approximately 8 times a year, our symposia bring physicians and advanced practice providers together to review peer-reviewed literature and clinical updates across GI subspecialty topics.

APP

APP Symposia

Hosted twice yearly and tailored to the clinical interests of nurse practitioners and physician assistants, covering focused updates in diagnosis and treatment.

PC

Primary Care Symposium

An annual half-day GI Update for community primary care providers, featuring expert-led sessions and CME credit, held at a conference venue in the Columbus area.

Who We Are

Backed by Central Ohio's GI expertise

OGG Edu is an independent 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Our educational programming draws on the clinical expertise of physicians and advanced practice providers from Ohio Gastroenterology Group, giving Central Ohio providers direct access to the specialists shaping GI and liver care in the region.
