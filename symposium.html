Annual GI Update

Primary Care Symposium

Our annual education update for primary care providers in the community covers important advances in the management of GI and liver disease. This half-day event is designed to empower primary care providers with the latest clinical information and give them the opportunity to interact directly with regional GI and hepatology experts. Topics span updates in viral hepatitis, PBC, cirrhosis, and MASLD, as well as GERD, H. pylori, pancreaticobiliary pathology, IBD, IBS, C. difficile, and colon cancer screening.
